New Feature Requests — Church Management App

Prepared for church management review  •  4 August 2026
This document summarizes new feature requests gathered from church management for the parish management app, intended for engineering scoping. Items marked TBD need confirmation from church management before detailed implementation planning can begin.

1Mobile App for All User Levels

All existing user roles need mobile access to the app, in addition to the current web access:

  • Church Admin, Unit Admin, Kudumbakutayima (household group) Admin, Member, and Donor.
  • Each role will continue to see only its own sub-level data — a church admin sees their church, a kudumbakutayima admin sees only their group's houses/members, a member sees only their own record — the same scoping already in place on the web app today.

2Approval Flow for Sthothrakazhcha & Spiritual Activities

A new two-step validation flow for weekly Sthothrakazhcha offerings and spiritual activity records:

  • Step 1 — Mark: the Kudumbakutayima Admin marks/logs the entry for their group.
  • Step 2 — Approve: church management reviews and approves the entry.
  • An entry only becomes valid (counted) once approved — marking alone is not sufficient.

Note: today the Kudumbakutayima Admin role is read-only; this request introduces a new, scoped ability for that role to mark these two entry types as pending approval.

3New Offering / Contribution Types

In addition to the existing Campaign feature, new contribution categories are requested:

Kurubana, Opice, VencherippNot separate types on their own — each is a category inside the broader Thirukkarmangal (Sacred Rites) master rate list. See Section 4 for the full list.

To confirm TBD for Pathavarm: whether it is compulsory or optional, whether the amount is per-house or per-member, and whether it is one-time or recurring.

6Hall Booking — Draw of Lots

For the hall booking deposit scheme, add a periodic draw of lots among contributors:

  • When a contributor's name is drawn, they no longer need to pay the remaining monthly installments starting from the following month.
  • Their deposit is treated as complete from the point of the draw onward.

To confirm TBD: how often draws happen, whether only up-to-date payers are eligible, and whether a contributor can be drawn more than once.
